# Visual Progression: Van's Hemogen Vials

A RimWorld 1.7 mod. Hemogen packs made before your colony reaches the industrial age are Van's hemogen vials. Packs
made after are the normal blood bags, named "hemogen pack" again. Old vials keep their look until they're used, so the
change happens slowly. With Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage, its animal, corpse and sanguophage packs change the
same way.

[Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3836592165)

Something has to raise your colony's tech level as you play, such as Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals. Without one,
your colony stays at the age it starts in, so its packs never change.

## How it works

It lists the hemogen packs for Visual Progression Framework, which gives each pack its look when it's made: Van's vial
up to the medieval age, the original blood bag from the industrial age on. It's XML only and adds no item art. It
points at Van's images and at the game's and Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage's own, so another retexture can't
replace either look.

Packs from hemogen extraction, VRE's extraction jobs and anything else your colony makes get your colony's age. Packs
that traders sell, and that other factions' pawns carry, follow that faction's age. Packs found on the map, quest
rewards and raid loot keep the normal art, which with Van's mod is the vial.

The name follows the look: "hemogen vial" or "hemogen pack". Storage filters, bills and info cards name the kind of
pack after your colony's current age. The description stays Van's text about the glass vial, since a look can't change
it.

## Adding and removing

- **Safe to add to an existing save.** Packs already made keep their look, and packs made after get the look of your
  colony's age.
- **Safe to remove.** Every pack goes back to Van's vials.

## Requirements

Requires [Visual Progression Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834891951) 1.1 or
later and [Van's Retexture : Hemogen Vials](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3453224032), which
needs Biotech. [Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2963116383)
is optional.
